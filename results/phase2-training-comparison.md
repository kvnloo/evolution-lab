## Headline verdict (read this first)

**The mushroom-body readout does not beat ridge or logistic on this corpus, and on the two most informative
held-out splits it is at or below the majority floor.**

| | validation | confirm | ood | sealed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mushroom vs ridge | tie (0.000 vs 0.000) | tie with ridge at 0.600, both **below the majority floor 0.800** | **mushroom loses** (0.667 vs 0.833) | tie (1.000 vs 1.000) |
| mushroom vs logistic | tie (0.000) | **mushroom loses** (0.600 vs 0.800) | tie (0.667 vs 0.667) | tie (1.000) |
| mushroom vs majority floor | tie (0.000) | **mushroom loses** (0.600 vs 0.800) | tie (0.667 vs 0.667) | **mushroom wins** (1.000 vs 0.000) |

In-distribution (5-fold CV inside the fit split) **all four learned models are perfect (1.000)** and the mushroom
architecture buys exactly nothing over a linear ridge readout on the same features. The one place mushroom is
above the floor is `sealed_human_audited` — 15 episodes, one gold class, a split I flag as degenerate below.

A linear model on the frozen state is sufficient. The biology-inspired encoder is not earning its place here.

### Why the held-out splits are largely uninformative

The split is **family-disjoint from train**: train sees 5 of 10 task families, and validation sees only `control_flow`,
`confirm` only `control_flow`+`security`, `ood` only the four families absent from train. Consequence, straight from the
data: 5 of the 11 held-out gold actions (`stop`, `continue`, `retry`, `deny`, `fetch`) **never appear as a train label**.
No train-only model can emit them except by tie-break against the fixed floor score I give unseen classes.

- `validation` is the worst case: every episode's gold is one of `stop`/`continue`/`retry` and **every episode's gold is
  absent from the train label set**. All five models score **0.000**, which is *below* the 0.333 uniform-candidate chance.
  The validation numbers are not a model ranking; they are an artifact of the split. **Do not read them as results.**
- `deny` in `confirm` is likewise unreachable (its candidate set is `{deny, fs.read}`, so every model answers `fs.read`).
- The one zero-shot action that is "recovered", `fetch` (ood), is recovered only because its candidate set is the
  singleton `{fetch}` — a forced answer, not generalization. This is why `accuracy_on_gold_absent_from_train = 1.000` for
  ood in the JSON: it is 15 forced singleton answers, nothing more.
- `sealed_human_audited` has one gold class, so its macro recall is identical to its accuracy (degenerate), and its
  accuracy is decided by the label-adjacent `features.expect_abstain`/family indicator. Marked uninformative.

## Identical representation and candidate sets

One deterministic numeric vector per episode, built only from `state_before`, 30 dims (`state_before_full` variant):
1. **6 continuous, z-scored with TRAIN mean/std**: `budget_units`, `features.authority_breadth`,
   `features.candidate_action_count`, `features.declared_dangerous_count`, `features.legal_family_count`,
   `features.satisfied_count`;
2. **4 binary 0/1**: `features.expect_abstain`, `features.gold_present`, `features.has_deterministic_solution`,
   `features.is_empty`;
3. one-hot `state_before.family` over the train family vocabulary + UNK (6);
4. multi-hot `features.legal_families` over the train union + UNK (8);
5. multi-hot `features.legal_risk_classes` over the train union + UNK (3);
6. one-hot `features.max_risk_class` over the train vocabulary + UNK (3).

Vocabularies and scaling constants come **from the train split only**. `legal_actions` is not a feature — it is the
candidate mask, applied identically to all five models as a masked argmax, so all models share both representation and
candidate sets. The global action space is the 30-action union of `legal_actions` over all episodes (structural, no gold
involved); only the 9 train gold actions ever receive supervision, and the other 21 get a fixed floor score
(exactly 0 for majority/ridge, `log(1e-12)` for logistic/mlp).

**Label-adjacency warning:** `features.expect_abstain` is exactly equivalent to `gold == abstain` on this corpus (45/45).
It is retained in the primary variant because it is part of the frozen observation every model receives, and an ablation
(`no_label_adjacent`, drops `expect_abstain` + `has_deterministic_solution`, 28 dims) is reported in the JSON. The
ablation changes nothing on validation/confirm/ood and only moves `sealed` (ridge 1.000→0.000, mushroom 1.000→0.000,
logistic/mlp stay 1.000 via the `abstention` family indicator).

## Hyperparameters

Fixed **a priori**; no search was run and no held-out split was consulted. Ridge: direct one-hot least squares over all
30 columns with L2 = 1e-2 (repo `ridge_fit`) plus a ones bias column. Logistic: multinomial softmax, C = 1.0, lbfgs,
max_iter 5000. MLP: one hidden layer of width 16, ReLU, Adam, alpha 1e-4, lr 1e-3, early stopping on an internal 20%
split **carved out of the train split only** (patience 30). Mushroom: the shipped `local-plasticity-000` genome values —
n_kc = 128, k_winners = 13 (10%), 20 epochs, lr 0.35, seed 0 — with the repo's own `_pn_features`, `_kc_codes`,
`_sparse_pn_kc`, `_local_plasticity_step_samples` and `_plasticity_train` called directly.

## What made the comparison partly invalid

1. **`validation` (45 episodes) is 100% zero-shot in its gold action.** All five models score 0.000, below the 0.333
   uniform-candidate chance. Any "ranking" there is meaningless. **Marked uninformative.**
2. **`sealed_human_audited` (15 episodes, < 20) has a single gold class** (`abstain`), so macro recall is degenerate and
   the split decides nothing about relative model quality. **Marked uninformative.**
3. **5 of 11 held-out gold actions are absent from the train label set** (`stop`, `continue`, `retry`, `deny`, `fetch`),
   because held-out families are disjoint from train families. This caps the achievable held-out accuracy for reasons
   that have nothing to do with model class.
4. **`features.expect_abstain` encodes `gold == abstain` exactly** and is part of the frozen input; the sealed result is
   partly (ridge, mushroom: entirely) attributable to it.
5. The 90-episode `ood` split is heterogeneous: 45 of its episodes have singleton candidate sets (`db.migrate`, `fetch`)
   that any model answers correctly by force, which inflates every model's ood number by ~0.5 of its accuracy.

Net: only `confirm` (75) and `ood` (90) carry any usable signal, and both are contaminated by 1, 3 and 5. On that signal
**the linear models match or beat the mushroom-body readout**, and the majority floor beats ridge and mushroom on `confirm`.

## Informational: train-internal 5-fold CV (fit split only, not a held-out result)
